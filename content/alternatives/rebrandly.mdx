## Why people look for a Rebrandly alternative

**Ten links a month.** A tight limit for anything beyond a trial. ndle counts active links instead: keep up to 100 at once.

**Ten QR codes a month.** Rebrandly's free plan makes 10 QR codes a month, with a watermark. ndle's free plan makes as many as you need, with ndle's small mark in the middle.

**Finding out about broken links.** Rebrandly records broken branded links in your domain settings, where you can see them if you look. ndle checks every link's page every 30 minutes and flags failures on your Monitoring page.

## Where Rebrandly is ahead

- **Changing a link's destination** after it's shared, on paid plans.
- **Team and enterprise features,** for organisations managing many brands.
- **Custom link endings.** ndle's links end in six random characters.

## Moving from Rebrandly

Rebrandly says its links don't expire unless you set a date or delete them, and keep working if you downgrade to a free account ([Rebrandly Support](https://support.rebrandly.com/en/articles/469550-how-long-does-a-rebrandly-link-last)). So there's no rush: start new links on ndle, and move the important ones as you update them.
