Short.io has one of the most generous free plans around: 1,000 branded links and 5 custom domains. If you need lots of links across lots of domains, it's a strong choice. ndle is for a different need: knowing that the page behind every link still works.

## ndle and Short.io's free plans, side by side

| | ndle free | Short.io free |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short links | 100 active | 1,000 in total, for the life of the account |
| Custom domains | 1 | 5 |
| Click data | 30 days: day, country, device, referrer, UTM campaign | 50,000 tracked clicks a month |
| Checks that your pages still work | Every 30 minutes | Not on its pricing page |
| Link expiry dates | Up to 30 days ahead | From the Pro plan |
| Cheapest paid plan | No paid plan yet | Hobby, $5/month |

Short.io's figures come from its [pricing page](https://short.io/pricing) and [help centre](https://docs.short.io/articles/features/features/how-to-set-up-link-expiration-or-temporary-url), as they read on 28 September 2026.

## Why people look for a Short.io alternative

**A link count that doesn't refill.** Short.io's free links are "issued once and can be used over the account lifetime", so 1,000 is the total, not a monthly allowance. ndle counts active links: remove old ones and your room comes back.

**Nothing watches the destination.** Short.io tracks clicks. ndle also checks the page behind every link every 30 minutes, and flags it on your Monitoring page when it returns a 404, times out or fails its certificate check.

## Where Short.io is ahead

- **More links and more domains** on the free plan: 1,000 links and 5 domains, against ndle's 100 active links and 1 domain.
- **A cheap first paid plan,** at $5 a month.
- **Custom link endings.** ndle's links end in six random characters.

## Moving from Short.io

Short.io says that if you cancel a paid plan, your account moves to the free plan and your links keep working within its limits ([Short.io](https://short.io/pricing)). Deleting the account removes the links, so keep it while old links are still in use, and move the important ones to ndle as you update them.
