A QR code on a menu, a flyer or a box has to work for as long as it's printed. Most that stop working weren't damaged. The short link inside them broke, or the page behind it did.

## Why printed QR codes stop working

- **A trial or plan ends.** Many free QR generators make codes that work only while your account is active. QR TIGER's free dynamic codes, for example, stop after 500 scans ([QR TIGER Help Center](https://qrtiger.helpscoutdocs.com/article/368-how-do-i-fix-a-qr-code-that-has-reached-its-scan-limit)).
- **An ad page appears.** Bitly now shows an ad before links from free accounts open, including links behind QR codes ([Bitly Support](https://support.bitly.com/hc/en-us/articles/32874287800333-Why-are-there-ads-on-my-links)).
- **The page moves.** A new website or a new menu address leaves the old one returning "404 Not Found".

There's more on each of these in [QR code stopped working? Check the destination first](/blog/qr-code-stopped-working).

## How ndle QR codes work

Every ndle link comes with a QR code. The code opens the short link, and the short link sends people to your page.

- **Free and unlimited.** There's no cap on how many codes you make, no trial, and no scan limit.
- **No ad page.** A scan goes straight to your page.
- **Your own domain.** The free plan includes one custom domain, so codes can open `go.yourbrand.com` instead of someone else's address.
- **Print-ready files.** Download each code as SVG or PNG, with ndle's small brand mark in the middle or no mark at all.
- **Scans you can count.** Every scan is a click in your stats, by day, country, device, referrer and UTM campaign.
- **No expiry.** Links on a free account don't expire unless you set an end date.

## Know when the page behind it breaks

A printed code can't tell you when its page is gone. ndle checks the page behind every link every 30 minutes. When it returns a 404, times out or fails its certificate check, the link is flagged on your Monitoring page within half an hour, rather than whenever a customer mentions it.

## What ndle doesn't do yet

Some QR generators let you change where a printed code goes. ndle doesn't yet: once a link is made, its destination is fixed. So point each code at a page you control and expect to keep, like `yourcafe.com/menu` rather than a file that will be replaced. If that page ever has to move, add a permanent (301) redirect from its old address, and every printed code keeps working.

Custom logos inside the code and custom link endings aren't on the free plan either.
